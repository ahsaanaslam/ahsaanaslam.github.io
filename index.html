Independently developed Deployed & in active use

Operations Management System

The company's internal coordination hub. Tasks, tickets, meetings, and suggestions flow through one system - so work gets assigned, tracked, and resolved instead of getting lost in email threads and verbal requests.

  • Task assignment with priorities & deadlines
  • Maintenance, machinery & ERP ticketing
  • Meeting management & scheduling
  • Memos, circulars & announcements
  • Complaints & suggestions handling
  • Configurable task & ticket workflows
ASP.NET Web FormsVB.NETSQL Server 2014Visual Studio 2022

The problem

In a manufacturing company, internal work easily scatters across verbal requests, phone calls, and email: tasks lose their owner and deadline, maintenance issues go unlogged, and management has no view of what is pending or who is responsible for it.

The solution

One coordination system inside the ERP where every piece of work becomes a trackable record. Tasks carry an owner, a priority, and a deadline; tickets are classified by type (building maintenance, machinery, ERP, product) and routed to the person responsible for that area; meetings, memos, and suggestions live in the same system instead of inboxes.

How I built it

Designed the data model and status workflows (created, responded, deferred, closed) on SQL Server 2014, with the UI and business logic in ASP.NET Web Forms and VB.NET. Setup screens let managers define their own task types, statuses, and ticket routing per location and machine, so the module adapts to the company without code changes. I handled requirements, development, deployment, user support, and full documentation as sole developer.

02
Independently developed Deployed & in active use

Human Resources & Payroll Management System

The system of record for the company's workforce - covering the full employee lifecycle from hiring to retirement, with payroll built on governed, approval-driven workflows rather than spreadsheets and paperwork.

  • Employee master data & records
  • Hiring & position management
  • Attendance, shifts & leave management
  • Payroll processing & salary policies
  • Termination & retirement workflows
  • Multi-level management approvals
ASP.NET Web FormsVB.NETSQL Server 2014Visual Studio 2022

The problem

Managing a workforce on paperwork and spreadsheets makes payroll slow and error-prone: attendance is hard to verify, salary rules are applied by hand, and there is no single, reliable record of an employee's history from hiring to exit, nor any enforced approval before sensitive changes.

The solution

A system of record for the entire employee lifecycle: hiring and positions, employee master data, attendance with shifts and leave policies, payroll, and termination/retirement, with sensitive actions gated behind multi-level management approvals (department manager, HR management, finance).

How I built it

Modeled the schema around a central employee master on SQL Server 2014, with ASP.NET Web Forms and VB.NET on top. Payroll is policy-driven: shifts, overtime rates, leave deadlines, and deduction rules are configured once in HR Setup and applied automatically, so calculations come from configuration rather than manual edits. Attendance supports both automatic capture (barcode / face ID) and controlled manual entry. Sole developer across schema, payroll logic, UI, deployment, and documentation.

03
Independently developed Deployed & in active use

Product Material Master System

A single source of truth for every item the company manufactures, purchases, or sells. Standardized item creation, classification, and costing feed clean, consistent data to every other module in the ERP.

  • Standardized item creation workflows
  • Inventory categories & classification
  • Vendor & in-house sourcing with costing
  • Bills of material & part structures
  • Physical data, units & HS codes
  • Pricing & cost valuation methods
ASP.NET Web FormsVB.NETSQL Server 2014Visual Studio 2022

The problem

When every department describes items its own way, an ERP fills with duplicate records, inconsistent codes, and unreliable costs. Purchasing, warehousing, production, and sales end up working from different versions of the same item, and no report can be trusted.

The solution

A single, governed source of truth for every item the company manufactures, purchases, or sells: a standardized creation workflow that generates consistent item numbers by category and segment, structured classification, sourcing and costing per item, bills of material, and physical data such as units, weights, and HS codes.

How I built it

Translated detailed written design specifications (shown in the gallery) into a normalized SQL Server 2014 schema, then built the tabbed creation workflow in ASP.NET Web Forms and VB.NET: item type and segment first, then sourcing and costing, bill of material, and physical data, with verify and approve steps before an item is posted. Other ERP modules read from this master, so clean data propagates across the whole platform. Sole developer from data model to deployment and documentation.

These are internal enterprise systems. All screens shown are sanitized - they contain no confidential business data, employee records, credentials, or proprietary details.
