I'm Ahsaan Aslam, a Computer Science undergraduate and software developer. For the past three years I've worked at Airsupply while pursuing my degree — building, deploying, and maintaining the enterprise software the company runs on every day.

My core work is ERP development: I independently designed and delivered three major modules — Operations Management, HR & Payroll, and Material Master — that are deployed and actively used across the organization. Beyond the ERP, I manage the company's IT and network infrastructure — troubleshooting networks, CCTV systems, hardware, and Microsoft Office environments — and I build business websites on Shopify and WordPress, including e-commerce and restaurant sites, plus freelance web projects.

Balancing full-time professional responsibilities with university studies has sharpened the way I work: disciplined time management, clear prioritization, and a habit of shipping software that people actually depend on.
